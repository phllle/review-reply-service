Frequently asked questions

How does Replyr work with Google?

You connect your Google Business Profile, and Replyr automatically replies to new reviews as the business owner. You choose the schedule and can turn it off anytime.

Is there a free trial, and how much is it after?

Yes — a 30-day free trial, then $19/month for the base plan. No credit card is required to start.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel anytime from the billing portal; auto-reply simply stops at the end of your billing period.

Will the replies sound like my business?

You add your own contact and can describe your business so replies fit your voice. For 1–3 star reviews you can enable preview mode, which holds the reply for 15 minutes and emails you a one-click cancel link before it posts.

What does Replyr Pro add?

Replyr Pro adds email and SMS campaigns (birthday, holiday and event, and one-off promos) plus review requests, sent to the customer list you upload. The message and offer are yours; sending is handled for you.

How do you handle SMS and email opt-outs?

Every campaign email includes an unsubscribe link and every SMS includes “Reply STOP to opt out.” We process opt-outs and do not message unsubscribed contacts. See our Messaging compliance page for details.
